About the TF series

The TF series is primarily a directional (trend-following) strategy series, with a minor non-directional stance built in. Please keep this character in mind when you judge its behaviour across different market conditions.

We run these strategies live in our own personal account as well, and keep working to optimise them further.

Expected drawdowns

Individual part8–12%
Part 1–4 combo8–10%
Suggested allocation≤ 10–15%of your total capital

These figures come from our past data analysis. They are indicative, not a ceiling: in adverse or unusual markets the actual drawdown can go deeper. Treat them as normal, expected behaviour for this style of strategy, and deploy only once you are comfortable with a larger drawdown in a worst case. Size your deployment strictly to your own risk appetite.

Running at your own discretion

If on a particular day you feel the strategy may not suit you, you are free to pause or adjust. Your returns may then differ from full everyday live deployment, and your drawdown may be more or less than ours.

Backtests

Each part is itself a blend of several strategies, so we don't share per-part backtests: that would expose the underlying logic and erode the edge for every subscriber. The combined Part 1–4 portfolio also cannot be backtested on Tradetron because of platform limits. If you'd like more evidence, watching the live daily P&L here for a while before deploying is often the wiser choice.

Percentages on this page are of deployed capital: ₹4L per part, ₹20L for all five parts combined. Daily P&L reflects our live deployment on Tradetron; your results may vary with slippage, broker API latency and costs.
