P&L is Tradetron's mark-to-market figure (includes open positions at their latest mark), not settled profit. History up to 30-Oct-2026 comes from Tradetron's reconciled daily data; from then on each day's figure is the last 15-minute reading of that day (GitHub can start a reading a few minutes late). Days where no TF part traded are not recorded. When no part has traded yet (evenings, weekends, holidays, early morning), the tiles keep showing the last session that traded, with its date and time; “Checked” shows when Tradetron was last asked. Percentages are of deployed capital: ₹4L per part, ₹20L combined (all five parts).
